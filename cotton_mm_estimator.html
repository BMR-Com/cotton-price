Data Sources

Roll weeks excluded from training: Jan last-biz to Feb 15 | Mar last-biz to Apr 15 | May last-biz to Jun 15 | Jul last-biz to Aug 15 | Oct last-biz to Nov 15
OI window: prior Tuesday through this Tuesday (cotDate-7 to cotDate). Delta = this-Tue OI minus prior-Tue OI = net change over exactly Wed-Thu-Fri-Mon-Tue, matching the COT reporting period.
Active contract for front-month OI is always taken from the COT Tuesday date (not per-day) to avoid FND transition mismatches mid-week.
OI features: front_oi_delta (front month, consistent contract) + comb_oi_delta (all contracts summed = total OI)
