Enhanced: COT week Wed–Tue | Daily OI aggregation | Front OI + Back OI (orthogonal) | Volume/OI transfer signal | Long + Short separately → Net | Auto-detects volume columns

📁 Data Sources

Volume columns: Auto-detected — any header containing "VOLUME" or "VOL" (e.g. CTMAR1 Comdty VOLUME). If absent, model falls back gracefully to OI + price only.
COT week: Wednesday open → Tuesday close (positions as of Tuesday).
OI split: Front month OI and Back months OI are kept separate (orthogonal — no collinearity).

⚙️ Model Settings

Transfer logic: High total volume + flat total OI on same day = spec↔commercial handoff. No net futures OI change but COT will show large repositioning. Captured as weekly count + direction score.
