🎯 Cotton Managed Money Position Estimator

Predict COT Managed Money Net Position changes from daily Open Interest movements | FND computed purely from exchange logic | Regression models: Front Month OI vs Combined OI

📁 Data Sources

FND Logic: ICE Cotton No. 2 — First Notice Day = 5 business days before the 1st business day of the delivery month. Front month auto-detected from OI data using contract month codes (MAR, MAY, JUL, OCT, DEC).

⚙️ Model Settings

COT reports released Friday for Tuesday positions. OI changes Mon–Fri predict next COT. Front month determined by FND: MAR→MAY→JUL→OCT→DEC cycle.
